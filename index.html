Open Flight

Fly a plane over the real world, pop balloons, chase drones and race through rings. Meta Quest, standalone.
Cesium setup: sign in at ion.cesium.com, open Asset Depot, search Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles and press Add to my assets. Then copy your Default token from Access Tokens.
Changes save on this device and apply straight away. Map and graphics changes apply the next time you start flying.
Free terrain: elevation from Terrain Tiles (Mapzen, AWS Open Data). Imagery: Sentinel-2 cloudless by EOX IT Services GmbH (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2020). Google 3D map data © Google.
